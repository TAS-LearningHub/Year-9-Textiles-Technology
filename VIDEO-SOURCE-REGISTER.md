# Year 9 Textiles video source register

Audit date: 18 August 2026; source decision updated 19 August 2026  
Scope: 24 named theory sections across Modules 1–8  
Authority: `work/source-contract-v2.md` plus current course theory

## Validation method

Each retained video was checked against its exact YouTube ID. The exact title and channel resolved through oEmbed-compatible metadata, its `hqdefault` thumbnail returned HTTP 200, and its `youtube-nocookie.com/embed/{id}` page returned HTTP 200 on 18 August 2026. Every player also keeps a normal YouTube fallback link because school filters and publisher settings can change.

## Rejected or removed material

- The former 13-card catalogue used generic labels such as `Designer source 1` and did not establish theory adjacency. It was replaced.
- Nine catalogue items with no defensible section match were removed from student view rather than relabelled.
- `Tbw1zpvBQB8` resolves to a screen-printing tutorial, so it was not retained as a generic evaluation or labelling source.
- `O8AF_tLLAxQ` resolved to a Spanish-language handmade-product costing clip and extended into business pricing beyond the source-bound student costing task.
- `sNjtXlqUX6Q` resolved to a Tamil-language machine lesson and could not replace the exact classroom-machine demonstration.
- `MZN-yjNKvNg` produced conflicting metadata (a current Canva portfolio tutorial rather than the cited FIT title), so it was rejected.
- `UOxgy8x0Ps0` was not retained because metadata validation returned an authorisation error even though a search result existed.

## Public-release boundary

This register approves the video layer for whole-course release. It does not authorise a Module 8 gift project, replace local WHS or practical demonstrations, or convert a generic video into task authority. Whole-course publication still depends on the independent release gates recorded outside this register.
