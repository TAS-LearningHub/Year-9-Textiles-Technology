NESA alignment

Syllabus expectations

Textiles Technology 7–10 (2019)

The task set combines practical project work, design documentation, textile-designer and historical investigation, textile materials and process knowledge, safe production and evaluation. Together, the four tasks assess the authorised TEX5 outcomes listed in the teacher-issued notices.

Design and investigate

Develop textile design ideas and investigate designers, eras, target markets and influences.

Produce safely

Select appropriate materials, techniques and equipment to produce quality textile items.

Document evidence

Communicate design development, production decisions, practical progress and evaluation.

Apply knowledge

Explain textile properties, patterns, design concepts and course learning in the final examination.
