# Year 9 Textiles — foundation history

This is the isolated Student Course Builder foundation. The first attempt remains unchanged in `year-9-textiles-course`.

## Foundation established

- A student-facing home page with a clear textiles identity and an eight-module pathway.
- A shared module shell that links the lesson, activity and folio evidence.
- Module 6 established the representative pattern: three learning sections, three purposeful diagrams, 30 knowledge checks, precise feedback and three scaffolded written responses.
- The original Magazine Studio activity supplied the evidence-planning pattern that was later rebuilt inside the complete Busy Work library.
- An eight-slide Module 6 lesson that opens, prints and downloads as a self-contained HTML file.
- Local-browser progress saving, accessible labels, keyboard-operable controls and print layouts.
- Desktop and exact 390 px mobile checks with no page overflow or broken images on the primary routes.

## Completion state

- The representative standard has been scaled across all eight modules.
- Module alignment, navigation, section references, presentations, graphics, videos, folio, assessment, teaching program and twenty-activity Busy Work passes are integrated.
- Assessment conflicts are reconciled in the assessment manifest without invented dates.
- Publication authority was confirmed on 19 August 2026; the release manifest records the actual commit, deployment and live-verification state.

## Controlling release boundary

The supplied 2024 Teacher Edition controls this source edition. Practical methods remain subject to teacher direction, Textiles as Gifts remains on hold until an approved brief is supplied, and later cohorts require formally issued replacement notices.
